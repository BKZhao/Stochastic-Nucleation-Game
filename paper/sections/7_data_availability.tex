\section*{Data availability}

The simulation code, the analysis and plotting sources, and the archived trajectory data supporting this article are available in the Stochastic-Nucleation-Game repository at \url{https://github.com/BKZhao/Stochastic-Nucleation-Game.git}. The archiving protocol and checksum records for the trajectory data, together with the current release status of the newer parameter-grid and continuation data, are described in Supplemental Material, Sec.~\ref{sm-sec:checks}.
